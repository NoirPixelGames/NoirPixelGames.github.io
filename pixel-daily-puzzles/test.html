Pixel Daily Puzzles — join the closed test

Classic logic puzzles — sudoku, nonogram, hashi and more. Three new levels every day, one streak to keep. Android · offline · 13+

Join the group and test it Türkçe

How to join (2 minutes)

  1. Join the test group. groups.google.com/g/noirpixel-testers → Join group, with the Google account your Play Store uses — that address is how Google identifies a tester.
    Rather not join a group? Mail the address to iletisim.noirpixel@gmail.com and I'll add it the same day.
  2. Opt in to the test.
    play.google.com/apps/testing/com.noirpixel.pixeldailypuzzles → Become a tester.
    If it says "app not available", your account is not on the list yet — try again a little later. Order matters.
  3. Install from Play and keep it for 14 days.
    play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.noirpixel.pixeldailypuzzles
    You don't have to play every day; staying installed is what counts. Google counts 12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days, and an uninstall resets the counter.

What is it?

  • A new set of levels every day — word puzzles and pictograms, with XP, levels and a streak you won't want to break.
  • A dozen classic puzzles — sudoku, killer sudoku, futoshiki, nonogram, hashi, slitherlink, numberlink, word search, minesweeper and more.
  • Three difficulties every day: easy, medium and hard, fresh each morning.
  • Fully offline. No connection needed to play — ever.
  • Archive: past days stay playable — open them with a rewarded ad, or with VIP.
  • Collection with a rarity mark: you see how many players finished a level.
  • Play Games achievements, a daily leaderboard and optional cloud save. Ads are optional (rewarded only) and VIP removes them.

During the test

  • If anything breaks, or a screen leaves you unsure what to do, tell me: iletisim.noirpixel@gmail.com. One sentence is enough; a screenshot is gold.
  • Draw & Rate and Top Answers have left this game — they now live in a separate app (Pixel Hype Puzzle). This one is all puzzles.
  • Please don't tap ads on purpose — Google treats that as invalid traffic on test accounts.
  • Purchase screens (VIP, hints) are in test mode: no real money is charged.
  • Test for test: if you're a developer in the same 12-tester situation, send me your opt-in or group link at the address above — I install the same day, keep it the full 14 days and report the bugs I hit.
  • I'll ping you when it goes live; your name can go on the thanks list if you want.

Nasıl katılırım? (2 dakika)

  2. Teste kaydol.
    play.google.com/apps/testing/com.noirpixel.pixeldailypuzzles → Test kullanıcısı ol.
    "Uygulama kullanılamıyor" diyorsa hesabın henüz listede değildir — biraz sonra tekrar dene, sıra önemli.
  3. Play'den kur ve 14 gün silme.
    play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.noirpixel.pixeldailypuzzles
    Her gün oynamak zorunda değilsin; kurulu kalması yeterli. Google'ın saydığı şey 12 kişinin 14 gün boyunca kurulu tutması, silen olursa sayaç başa döner.
