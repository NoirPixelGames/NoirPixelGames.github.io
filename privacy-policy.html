Reklam kişiselleştirmesini Android ayarlarından ("Reklam kimliğini sıfırla / kişiselleştirmeyi kapat") veya oyun içi "Gizlilik seçenekleri" düğmesinden değiştirebilirsiniz. Analitik toplamaya cihaz düzeyinde itiraz için uygulamayı kaldırmanız yeterlidir; cihazdaki tüm oyun verisi de silinir.

Veriler Google'ın altyapısında şifreli bağlantılarla iletilir ve saklanır. Oyun kayıt dosyası cihazınızda şifrelenmiş olarak tutulur.

Pixel Roll Dungeon — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-09-16 · Developer: Noir Pixel Games · Contact: iletisim.noirpixel@gmail.com

This policy explains what data the Pixel Roll Dungeon mobile game published on Google Play (package com.noirpixel.pixelrolldungeon, the "Game") processes, why, and what your rights are. The Game does not require an account and does not collect identifying information such as your name, e-mail address or phone number.

1. Data we process

DataSourcePurpose
Game progress (gold, unlocked dice, records, settings)The Game itselfStored only on your device; never sent to a server. Deleted when you uninstall.
App instance ID, device model, OS version, country, in-game events (run started/ended, chapter cleared, ad watched, etc.)Google Firebase AnalyticsUnderstanding how the Game is played and improving its balance. Events contain no personal information.

2. Sharing

Data is shared only with the service providers above (Google LLC and its ad partners) under their own policies. We do not sell your data or hand it to third parties for marketing. Relevant policies: Google Privacy Policy, Firebase, AdMob.

3. Retention

Analytics and crash data is retained by Google Firebase for at most 14 months. Advertising data is subject to the partners' policies. Game progress stays on your device until you delete it.

4. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA / KVKK)

You have the right to know what data is processed about you, to request correction or deletion, and to object to processing. Write to iletisim.noirpixel@gmail.com. You can change ad personalisation in your Android settings ("Reset advertising ID / opt out of personalisation") or through the in-game "Privacy options" button. To stop analytics collection at the device level, uninstall the Game; this also deletes all local game data.

6. Security

Data is transmitted and stored on Google's infrastructure over encrypted connections. The local save file is stored encrypted on your device.

7. Changes

When this policy changes, the effective date is updated and material changes are announced in the Game.
