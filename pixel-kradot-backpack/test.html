Kradot Backpack — join the closed test

A dark-fantasy backpack auto-battler: pack your bag, fuse items, watch your hero fight. Android · offline

Join the group and test it Türkçe

How to join (2 minutes)

  1. Join the test group. groups.google.com/g/noirpixel-testers → Join group, with the Google account your Play Store uses — that address is how Google identifies a tester.
    Already in the group from one of our other games? Then you're set, skip to step 2.
  2. Opt in to the test.
    play.google.com/apps/testing/com.noirpixel.kradotbackpack → Become a tester.
    If it says "app not available", your account is not on the list yet — try again a little later. Order matters.
  3. Install from Play and keep it for 14 days.
    play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.noirpixel.kradotbackpack
    You don't have to play every day; staying installed is what counts. Google counts 12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days, and an uninstall resets the counter.

What is it?

  • Your bag is your build. Items take space on a grid; neighbours buff each other, rotation and placement matter. You don't steer the fight — the bag you packed does.
  • 85 items, 24 fusions. Put a dagger next to a vampire fang and a Vampiric Dagger is born. Poison, bleed, seals, thorns and Blood Rage feed one another.
  • Four heroes. Jarlkor the shield master, bloodthirsty Falkum, Radav of the seals and Morvath the poisoner — each wants a different bag.
  • 15 trophies to the Usurper King. Field anomalies bend the rules; opponents are other players' winning bags. The Eternal Backpack keeps upgrades between runs.
  • English, Türkçe, Português, Deutsch · colour-blind modes, text size, reduce motion.

During the test

  • If anything breaks or a screen leaves you unsure what to do, tell us: iletisim.noirpixel@gmail.com. One sentence is enough; a screenshot is gold.
  • The game changes during the test. New art, balance passes and features land as updates — keep it installed and they arrive automatically.
  • Please don't tap ads on purpose — ads are test ads, and Google treats deliberate taps on test accounts as invalid traffic.
  • Purchase screens (Remove Ads, Revive) are in test mode: no real money is charged.
  • Test for test: if you're a developer in the same 12-tester situation, send us your opt-in or group link — we install the same day and keep it the full 14 days.

Gruba katıl ve testçi ol

Nasıl katılırım? (2 dakika)

  1. Test grubuna katıl. groups.google.com/g/noirpixel-testers → Gruba katıl. Telefonundaki Play Store'un kullandığı Google hesabıyla katıl; Google testçiyi o adresten tanıyor.
    Diğer oyunlarımızdan dolayı grupta zaten varsan 2. adıma geç.
  2. Teste kaydol.
    play.google.com/apps/testing/com.noirpixel.kradotbackpack → Test kullanıcısı ol.
    "Uygulama kullanılamıyor" diyorsa hesabın henüz listede değildir — biraz sonra tekrar dene, sıra önemli.
  3. Play'den kur ve 14 gün silme.
    play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.noirpixel.kradotbackpack
    Her gün oynamak zorunda değilsin; kurulu kalması yeterli. Google 12 kişinin 14 gün boyunca kurulu tutmasını sayıyor, silen olursa sayaç başa döner.
