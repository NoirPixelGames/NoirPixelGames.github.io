How to join (2 minutes)

  1. Join the test group. groups.google.com/g/noirpixel-testers → Join group, with the Google account your Play Store uses — that address is how Google identifies a tester.
    Rather not join a group? Mail the address to iletisim.noirpixel@gmail.com and I'll add it the same day.
  2. Opt in to the test.
    play.google.com/apps/testing/com.noirpixel.pixelrolldungeon → Become a tester.
    If it says "app not available", your account is not on the list yet — try again a little later. Order matters.
  3. Install from Play and keep it for 14 days. (~140 MB)
    play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.noirpixel.pixelrolldungeon
    You don't have to play; staying installed is what counts. Google counts 12 testers opted in for 14 continuous days, and an uninstall resets the counter.

What is it?

  • 14 dice types: fire splashes, ice freezes, poison ticks for turns, the sniper one-shots on a six, the mimic copies its neighbour.
  • 70+ relics with real synergies; three biomes, four difficulties, nine enemy classes.
  • The Endless Gate and a Daily Dungeon (same seed for everyone, one attempt), plus an ELO arena.
  • Offline, optional ads, no pay-to-win.

During the test

  • If anything breaks, or a screen leaves you unsure what to do, tell me: iletisim.noirpixel@gmail.com. One sentence is enough; a screenshot is gold.
  • Please don't tap ads on purpose — Google treats that as invalid traffic on test accounts.
  • Purchase screens are in test mode: no real money is charged.
  • Test for test: if you're a developer in the same 12-tester situation, send me your opt-in or group link at the address above — I install the same day, keep it the full 14 days and report the bugs I hit.
  • I'll ping you when it goes live; your name can go on the thanks list if you want.

Nasıl katılırım? (2 dakika)

  1. Test grubuna katıl. groups.google.com/g/noirpixel-testers → Gruba katıl. Telefonundaki Play Store'un kullandığı Google hesabıyla katıl; Google testçiyi o adresten tanıyor.
    Grup istemiyorsan adresini iletisim.noirpixel@gmail.com adresine yolla, aynı gün listeye eklerim.
  2. Teste kaydol.
    play.google.com/apps/testing/com.noirpixel.pixelrolldungeon → Test kullanıcısı ol.
    "Uygulama kullanılamıyor" diyorsa hesabın henüz listede değildir — biraz sonra tekrar dene, sıra önemli.
  3. Play'den kur ve 14 gün silme. (~140 MB)
    play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.noirpixel.pixelrolldungeon
    Oynamak zorunda değilsin; kurulu kalması yeterli. Google'ın saydığı şey 12 kişinin 14 gün boyunca kurulu tutması, silen olursa sayaç başa döner.
